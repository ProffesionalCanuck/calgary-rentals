Mid-August is Calgary's peak month (Stampede is over, but summer rates hold). Booking the same week runs roughly $35–60 a hostel bed, $120–180 a decent hotel room, $150–250 a full apartment.

💰 Cheapest for one person

Solo travel is where hostels beat everything — a private hostel room often undercuts the worst hotel in town.
Hostelworld — Calgary $35–60
Every Calgary hostel with live availability and reviews. Filter to Private Room if you don't want a dorm — usually lands $100–140 and still beats a hotel.
Open Hostelworld →
HI Calgary City Centre Top pick
Downtown, walkable to everything, full kitchen, private rooms as well as dorms — consistently the best-reviewed budget bed in the city, from about $33. To skip platform fees, book it direct at hihostels.ca instead.

🏘️ Airbnb-style, without Airbnb

Whole-apartment platforms. Smaller inventory in Calgary than Booking or Vrbo, but sometimes cheaper because guest fees are lower or zero.
Vrbo — Beltline
177 apartments and 90 condos in the Beltline alone — the walkable bar-and-restaurant strip just south of downtown. Add your dates and 1 guest on the page.
Open Beltline rentals →
Extended-stay suites from ~$82
Kitchenette apartments that also take 2-night bookings — the Airbnb feel (cook your own food, do laundry) with a front desk. Cheapest apartment-style option in the city.
Open Expedia list →
Houfy $0 guest fee
Book direct with the owner, no service fee — versus the 12–16% Airbnb tacks on. Search Calgary once it loads. Inventory is thin here, so treat it as a bonus check, not your main search.
Open Houfy →
Whimstay Last-minute
Discounted unsold vacation rentals inside a short booking window — built exactly for a "two nights, soon" trip. Search Calgary once it loads.
Open Whimstay →
Hotels.com
Same pool as Expedia but its own sale pricing, plus the stamp/reward-night program if you travel often. Always cross-check against Expedia.
Open Hotels.com →
Kijiji — Short-Term
Private hosts renting nightly and weekly, no platform in the middle. Cheapest option that isn't a hostel — but you're paying a stranger directly, so meet in person and never wire money ahead.

🛎️ Three specific places to check first

If you'd rather just pick one and be done. Booking direct with the hotel is often $10–25/night cheaper than the platforms once you take their member rate.
Sandman Signature Downtown ~$116–165
Big reliable downtown room, 5 min from the Saddledome, indoor pool and gym. Their RSVP Rewards signup takes up to 35% off the standard rate when you book 14+ days ahead — this is their live deals page.

Residence Inn — Beltline 9.6 rated
Full kitchen in every suite, free hot breakfast, rooftop garden, 24h gym. The closest a hotel gets to an apartment stay — good if you'd rather not eat out for two days straight.
